# Saccosphere Product Blueprint

## The Core Thesis

Saccosphere is not just a SACCO mobile app.

Saccosphere is a **multi-SACCO digital operating network** that brings together what is currently fragmented across:

- Individual SACCO apps.
- Legacy core banking systems.
- M-Pesa/paybill workflows.
- Manual KYC and paper forms.
- Spreadsheet-based loan tracking.
- Standalone SMS tools.
- Separate reporting tools.
- Disconnected member records.
- Single-SACCO portals that cannot see beyond one institution.

The opportunity is not merely to digitize one SACCO. The opportunity is to create the shared infrastructure layer that lets SACCOs operate, compete, report, and serve members digitally without each SACCO building and maintaining its own expensive technology stack.

The product thesis:

> Saccosphere turns Kenya's fragmented SACCO ecosystem into one connected digital network where members can discover, join, transact, borrow, repay, guarantee, and monitor multiple SACCO relationships from one place, while SACCOs get modern operations, compliance, payments, and oversight tools without building everything alone.

## Why This Market Is Real

Kenya's regulated SACCO sector is large enough to support a serious company.

Current sector indicators from the 2024 SASRA supervision cycle and sector reporting show:

- About **355 regulated SACCOs**.
- About **7.39 million members**.
- About **KES 1.07 trillion** in assets.
- About **KES 749 billion** in deposits.
- Thousands of smaller unregulated and community SACCOs outside the regulated count.

The market is also changing. SACCOs are being pushed toward:

- Digital member channels.
- Better reporting.
- Stronger governance.

- More transparent member services.
- Shared digital infrastructure.

This means SACCO software is not a nice-to-have. It is becoming operational infrastructure.

## The Problem Saccosphere Solves

SACCOs face two simultaneous problems:

1. Members now expect digital convenience similar to banks and fintech apps.
2. Most SACCOs cannot afford to build, secure, maintain, and continuously upgrade a full technology stack alone.

This creates fragmentation:

- Big SACCOs may have their own apps.
- Small SACCOs may rely on manual records.
- Some use legacy core systems.
- Some rely heavily on M-Pesa and spreadsheets.
- Members with multiple SACCO relationships must juggle separate apps, logins, statements, and loan records.
- No single SACCO can build a cross-SACCO view because each SACCO only sees itself.

Saccosphere's answer is not to become a clone of every SACCO's app. It is to become the shared layer above them.

## The Amica Objection

A likely challenge is:

> "Some SACCOs already have good apps and websites, like Amica. Why does Saccosphere matter?"

The answer:

> Amica proves SACCO digitization works. It does not disprove Saccosphere. It proves that members want digital SACCO services, but it also shows the limitation of single-SACCO apps: Amica's app serves Amica only. Saccosphere serves the member across SACCOs and gives smaller SACCOs access to digital infrastructure they could not afford alone.

Large SACCOs such as Amica can afford mobile banking, agency banking, Visa card access, PesaLink, and a polished digital stack. Most SACCOs cannot.

So the market splits into two groups:

- Large SACCOs with their own digital channels.
- The much larger long tail of SACCOs that need shared infrastructure.

Saccosphere serves the second group first, but still has a role for the first group:

- Discovery and member acquisition.
- Cross-SACCO credit/reputation signals.
- Compliance reporting layer.
- Shared fraud intelligence.
- Multi-SACCO member dashboard.
- Platform integrations that a single-SACCO app has no incentive to build.

One-line response:

> Amica's app proves individual SACCOs can go digital. Saccosphere is the network layer that connects SACCOs and members beyond one institution.

## What Makes Saccosphere Defensible

The moat is not the code alone.

The moat is the network.

Once SACCOs, members, payment activity, KYC records, loan repayment patterns, guarantor behavior, and compliance workflows begin living on one multi-SACCO platform, the product becomes more valuable with every SACCO added.

This gives Saccosphere potential network effects:

- More SACCOs create more member choice.
- More members create more value for SACCOs.
- More cross-SACCO repayment history improves credit signals.
- More transactions improve fraud detection.
- More SACCOs make compliance tooling cheaper per SACCO.
- More shared infrastructure reduces the cost of innovation for every SACCO.

The real moat:

> Single-SACCO systems solve record-keeping. Saccosphere builds a network.

## The Honest Ceiling

Some gaps cannot be solved by code alone.

| Blocked Item | Why It Requires A Partner |
|---|---|
| Physical or virtual card issuance | Needs card program manager, Visa/Mastercard rails, and PCI-DSS compliance. |
| Real PesaLink or interbank transfers | Needs IPSL or bank sponsor relationship. |
| Real CRB checks | Needs commercial contract with Metropol, TransUnion, or similar bureau. |
| Underwritten insurance/bima products | Needs licensed insurer as risk carrier. |
| Central Liquidity Facility and deposit guarantee fund integration | Sector-level infrastructure is still being built. |
| Official SASRA e-return submission | Needs SASRA-side portal/API access and credentials. |

This is important because Saccosphere should not pretend to control infrastructure it does not control.

Instead, the strategy is:

> Build everything software can solve now, and design clean integration points for the items that require partners later.

## The Product Journey To 90%+

The path to becoming market-leading is not to copy every competitor feature blindly. It is to close the biggest market gaps using Saccosphere's network advantage.

### Phase 1: FOSA Feel Without Bank Partnerships

#### 1. Paybill Account-Number Mapping

Use Safaricom Daraja C2B validation and confirmation to map each member account to the SACCO's existing paybill.

This allows members to deposit using paybill and account number even without opening the app.

Why it matters:

- Supports feature-phone and low-smartphone users.
- Gives SACCOs a bank-like deposit flow.
- Uses infrastructure many SACCOs already have.
- Reduces dependency on app-only payment journeys.

#### 2. Withdraw-To-M-Pesa

Extend the existing B2C disbursement infrastructure to support member-initiated withdrawals.

Why it matters:

- Gives members a practical cash-out path.
- Creates FOSA-like convenience without cards.
- Uses existing M-Pesa rails instead of needing Visa/Mastercard.

#### 3. Internal Transfers

Support ledger-to-ledger transfers between a member's own SACCO accounts:

- BOSA to FOSA.
- FOSA to loan repayment.
- Savings to welfare fund.
- Dividend to savings.

Why it matters:

- Solves many practical banking needs internally.
- Requires no external banking partnership.
- Improves member convenience.

### Phase 2: Sector-Specific SACCO Verticals

Not all SACCOs are the same. Saccosphere can become stronger by serving different SACCO types directly.

#### 4. Agricultural SACCOs

Add seasonal and harvest-linked loan products:

- Balloon repayments.
- Harvest-date schedules.
- Input-financing tracking.
- Produce-linked loan notes.

#### 5. Transport SACCOs

Add vehicle and route compliance:

- Vehicle plate.
- Route.
- PSV license expiry.
- Insurance expiry.
- Inspection due date.
- Owner-member link.
- Reminder notifications.
- Vehicle collateral linkage.

#### 6. Housing And Investment SACCOs

Add property and asset registers:

- Title/reference.
- Asset valuation.
- Ownership share.
- Development loan linkage.
- Financing status.

#### 7. Welfare And Mutual-Aid Funds

Add welfare fund support:

- Member contributions.
- Claim submission.
- Admin review.
- B2C payout.
- Welfare statements.

This is not underwritten insurance. It is a formalized version of welfare funds many SACCOs already run informally.

### Phase 3: Loan Risk And Collections

#### 8. Internal Cross-SACCO Credit Score

Build an internal reputation score from platform-owned data:

- On-time repayments.
- Missed repayments.
- Active loans.
- Historical defaults.
- Guarantor behavior.
- Multi-SACCO repayment patterns.

This should be clearly labeled as an internal platform score, not a CRB score.

Why it matters:

- It gives SACCOs a risk signal without waiting for a CRB contract.
- It uses Saccosphere's network advantage.
- It becomes more accurate as more SACCOs join.

#### 9. Automated Repayment Reminders And Collections

Add scheduled reminders for:

- Upcoming due dates.
- Missed repayments.
- Guarantor exposure alerts.
- STK push repayment requests.
- Admin collection queues.

Why it matters:

- Manual chasing is expensive.
- NPLs are rising.
- Timely reminders reduce default risk.

#### 10. Loan Restructuring And Top-Ups

Add admin workflows for:

- Rescheduling.
- Term extension.
- Penalty waiver.
- Top-up requests.
- Restructuring approval.

Why it matters:

- SACCOs regularly restructure loans.
- This avoids forcing every case into a new loan application.

### Phase 4: Governance, Fraud, Compliance, And Treasury

#### 11. AGM And Governance Module

Support:

- Board resolutions.
- Member voting.
- Election records.
- Committee positions.
- One-member-one-vote tallying.

Why it matters:

- SACCOs are member-owned cooperatives.
- Governance is central, not optional.

#### 12. Treasury And Investment Tracking

Support recording of:

- Fixed deposits.
- T-bills.
- Real estate investments.
- Idle funds.
- Liquidity positions.

Why it matters:

- Helps SACCOs monitor liquidity and balance-sheet health.
- Supports management reporting.

#### 13. SASRA-Format Return Generator

Generate exportable reports from platform data:

- Deposits.
- Loans.
- Membership.
- Capital.
- Liquidity.
- NPL.
- Income.
- Dividends.

Important distinction:

> Saccosphere can generate regulator-ready files from its own data without needing SASRA API access. Official portal submission is a later integration if SASRA provides access.

#### 14. Fraud And AML Rules Engine

Flag suspicious patterns:

- Large sudden withdrawals.
- Repeated failed logins.
- KYC mismatch on high-value transactions.
- Duplicate IDs.
- Disbursements to suspicious accounts.
- Rapid account detail changes before withdrawal.
- Multiple failed STK attempts.

Why it matters:

- Fraud and weak governance are real sector problems.
- Saccosphere already captures enough data to start rules-based detection.

## Business Model

Saccosphere should make money from SACCOs, not individual members.

The member app should remain free.

Revenue model:

- Per-SACCO subscription.
- Pricing scaled by member count.
- Optional transaction-based platform fee.
- Onboarding/migration fee for larger SACCOs.
- Premium compliance/reporting modules.
- Premium analytics and fraud/risk modules.
- Payment onboarding and platform operations fees.

Why this works:

- A custom app is expensive upfront.
- A shared platform spreads cost across many SACCOs.
- The more SACCOs join, the cheaper innovation becomes per SACCO.
- SACCOs pay because the platform reduces manual work, fraud risk, reporting burden, and member support cost.

## Regulatory Position

Saccosphere should not hold member funds.

Funds should move directly:

- From member to SACCO paybill/till.
- From SACCO B2C wallet/account to member.
- Through SACCO-owned payment credentials.

This keeps Saccosphere as a technology platform, not a deposit-taking institution.

Positioning:

> Saccosphere provides software, workflow, audit, reporting, and integration infrastructure. The licensed SACCO remains the regulated financial institution and holder of member funds.

## Why Saccosphere Can Become Hard To Beat

Saccosphere becomes hard to beat if it executes the network strategy before competitors reposition.

The defensibility comes from:

- Multi-SACCO member identity.
- Cross-SACCO activity history.
- Cross-SACCO internal credit scoring.
- Shared fraud intelligence.
- Shared compliance tooling.
- Marketplace/discovery layer.
- Payment onboarding infrastructure.
- SACCO admin and super-admin oversight.
- Lower cost per SACCO through shared infrastructure.

Competitors can copy screens. They cannot instantly copy a live multi-SACCO network with shared data, member adoption, SACCO trust, and operational history.

## The Pitch

### Short Version

> Saccosphere is building the shared digital operating network for SACCOs. Instead of every SACCO paying to build and maintain its own app, payment system, reporting tools, and admin workflows, Saccosphere gives them one platform for member onboarding, KYC, savings, loans, guarantors, M-Pesa payments, reporting, compliance, and multi-SACCO member access.

### Investor Version

> Kenya's SACCO sector manages over KES 1 trillion in regulated assets, but digital infrastructure is fragmented. Large SACCOs can afford custom apps; most cannot. Members often belong to multiple SACCOs, yet every SACCO system is isolated. Saccosphere solves this by creating a multi-SACCO digital operating network: one member app, one SACCO operations portal, and one platform oversight layer. We are starting with the daily workflows every SACCO needs, then building the network effects competitors cannot easily replicate: cross-SACCO discovery, reputation scoring, fraud signals, compliance tooling, and shared payment infrastructure.

### Response To Competition

> Existing SACCO systems mostly digitize one SACCO at a time. Saccosphere connects SACCOs into a network. A single-SACCO app cannot show a member's full cooperative financial life, cannot generate cross-SACCO credit signals, and cannot create a neutral SACCO discovery marketplace. That is the gap Saccosphere fills.

### Response To "Some SACCOs Already Have Apps"

> That proves the demand. But those apps are silos. Saccosphere is not trying to replace every strong SACCO app on day one. It gives the rest of the market affordable digital infrastructure, and it gives even mature SACCOs access to a network layer they cannot build alone: discovery, cross-SACCO intelligence, compliance tools, and multi-SACCO member visibility.
